# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in CapacityAvailability to be welcoming, respectful, and
free from harassment for everyone, regardless of background or experience.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and considerate of other contributors.
- Welcome different perspectives and technical approaches.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Respect privacy and report security issues through private channels.

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, threats, and publishing someone
else's private information without permission are not acceptable.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies to project issues, pull requests, discussions, and other
spaces where someone represents this project. The maintainer may edit or remove
content, close discussions, or restrict participation when necessary.

Please report conduct concerns privately to **alkinbg@gmail.com**. Reports
will be handled discreetly where practical, with attention to the safety of
everyone involved.
